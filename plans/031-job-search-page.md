## The angle, and why it is not the homepage toolkit again

The homepage already names all ten tools with descriptions
(Decide/Apply/Interview/Organize). Repeating that here would have been
duplicate feature copy on two pages. The strategy doc's actual job for
this page is "communicate why Offboard is different from a bag of AI
tools", so the page's argument is **the loop, and the fact that the loop
has a memory**:

- "A bag of tools makes you the integration" (the contrast band).
- The eight-step lifecycle, where step eight feeds step one: find, decide,
  research, build, track, prepare, record, and Career Context improves.
- The four stages appear compactly, framed by what each stage *decides*
  rather than by what each tool does, with the tool names as a single
  line. No homepage description is reused.
- Closes on the memory: every stage reads from and writes to Career
  Context, which is what makes the loop compound.
