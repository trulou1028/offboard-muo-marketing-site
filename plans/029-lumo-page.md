# Plan 029 — Lumo page (/lumo)

Phase 2, second item, of the site IA roadmap (plan 026). Built 2026-08-31.

## The angle

Lumo already appears in four places (homepage § 1, `/how-it-works` § 2,
`/career-context` § 10, `/integrations` § 11), so this page deliberately
takes the one angle none of them do: **what Lumo actually knows, what to
ask it, and why that differs from pasting a resume into a general
assistant.** It does not repeat any existing Lumo headline.

Sections: hero → what it knows (8 sources) → what to ask (8 prompts) →
the difference (contrast grid) → what it will not do → prefer your own
assistant → final CTA. Existing patterns only, no new classes.

## The honesty section is the point

Per the strategy doc, the differentiator is *not* "Lumo is smarter than
ChatGPT" — the page says so in as many words ("Not a smarter chatbot. A
better starting point"). The claim is only about what it works from.

`What Lumo will not do` reuses copy that is already shipped and verified
rather than inventing reassurance: the human-verified benefit facts line
(`/how-it-works` § 2), the independence disclaimer (canonical short form),
and the review-and-judgment line (PRODUCT_FAQS). The Free/Pro message
limit is a ledger fact; the ledger's "Appears on" column was updated to
include this page in the same edit.

## Band rhythm

deep → paper → mist → paper → forest → sand → forest → footer. Verified
with the band audit re-created from plan 028's table: **zero** adjacent
same-background bands site-wide, on this page and every other.
