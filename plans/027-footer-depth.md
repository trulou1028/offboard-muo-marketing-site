# Plan 027 — Give the footer its own depth

Owner report (2026-08-31): "many of the pages have the last section before
the footer be green and it flows right into the footer green instead of
standing on its own."

Correct, and it was a consequence of plan 023: Civic Modern ships two
greens, so `--mh-footer` was mapped to forest-deep. Every route ends on a
dark band — `.mh-final-cta` (forest) on how-it-works/pricing/resources,
`.mh-route-contact` (forest-deep) on about/public-partners/employers, and
`.mh-final2` (forest-deep) on the homepage — so on half the site the
footer was the *same* color as the section above it, with no edge at all.

## Fix

A deliberate third green: `--mh-footer: #07160c`, deeper than forest-deep
`#0f2617`, plus a top hairline (`--mh-hairline-on-dark`) so the edge stays
crisp where the step alone is subtle. Footer text contrast improves
(paper on the new fill is ~17:1; nothing in the footer falls below AA).

The owner's alternative — restructuring pages so they do not end on green
— stays available, but the footer color fixes every route at once without
touching band order.

## Verification note worth keeping

The first recapture silently left all 12 desktop baselines stale: the
dark-on-dark shift fell under Playwright's per-pixel color threshold, so
`--update-snapshots` saw no diff and rewrote nothing, while mobile and
tablet (where the added hairline reflowed content) did update. The live
desktop render was correct all along — verified by reading the computed
`background-color` in a browser, not by trusting the screenshots. The
baselines were then force-regenerated by deleting them first.

This is the trap AGENTS.md already names: "the visual suite runs at 1%
pixel tolerance, so a color change on a small element does NOT move a
baseline... inspect directly."
